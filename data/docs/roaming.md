# Roaming with HexaMobile

## Roaming in the EU / EEA
Thanks to "Roam Like At Home" rules, using your phone in EU and EEA countries is
included in your plan at no extra cost. Your data, calls, and SMS are drawn from
your normal monthly allowance. A fair-use data cap may apply on very large plans
when you are travelling for extended periods.

## Roaming outside the EU / EEA
Outside the EU/EEA (for example the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States,
or Morocco), standard roaming charges apply unless you buy a travel pass.

- **Pay-as-you-go data**: €10 per GB in Zone 1, €15 per GB in Zone 2.
- **Voice**: €0.50–€1.20 per minute depending on zone.
- **SMS**: €0.10 per message sent.

## Travel Passes (recommended for non-EU travel)
- **World Pass 1 GB** — €9.99, valid 7 days, includes 30 minutes of calls.
- **World Pass 5 GB** — €29.99, valid 30 days, includes 120 minutes of calls.

Buy a travel pass in the HexaMobile app before you leave, or on arrival. Passes
activate the first time you connect abroad.

## Turning roaming on or off
Data roaming is enabled by default in the EU/EEA and disabled outside it to protect
you from unexpected charges. You can change this any time under **Settings > Roaming**
in the HexaMobile app.
