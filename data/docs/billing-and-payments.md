# Billing and payments

## Your billing cycle
Postpaid customers are billed once a month. Your bill covers your monthly plan fee
plus any extra charges (data overage, roaming, or travel passes) from the previous
period. Bills are issued on the same calendar day each month and are available in
the HexaMobile app under **Billing > My invoices**.

## Payment methods
- SEPA direct debit (default and recommended)
- Credit or debit card
- One-off top-ups for prepaid lines

## Data overage charges
On capped plans, if you exceed your monthly data allowance you are charged
**€2 per additional GB**. Unlimited Max and prepaid plans never incur overage: on
prepaid, data simply stops when the allowance is used up until you top up.

## Late or missed payments
If a payment fails, HexaMobile retries the direct debit after 5 days. Your line
stays active during this grace period. After two failed attempts your outgoing
service may be suspended until the balance is paid. There is no late fee, but
repeated late payments can affect your eligibility for plan upgrades.

## Disputing a charge
If you believe a charge is incorrect, open a billing ticket in the app within 60
days of the invoice date. Most billing disputes are resolved within 48 hours.
