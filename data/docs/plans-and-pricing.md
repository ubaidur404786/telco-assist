# HexaMobile Plans and Pricing

HexaMobile offers both prepaid (no commitment) and postpaid (monthly) plans. All
prices include VAT and are billed in euros.

## Prepaid plans
- **Prepaid Mini** — €4.99/month. 2 GB data, 120 voice minutes, 200 SMS.
- **Prepaid Plus** — €9.99/month. 20 GB data, unlimited calls and SMS.

Prepaid plans have no contract and can be stopped at any time by simply not
topping up.

## Postpaid plans
- **Essential 5GB** — €12.99/month. 5 GB data, unlimited calls and SMS.
- **Smart 50GB** — €19.99/month. 50 GB data, unlimited calls and SMS.
- **Smart 100GB** — €24.99/month. 100 GB data, unlimited calls and SMS.
- **Unlimited Max** — €39.99/month. Unlimited data, calls, and SMS.
- **Family 200GB** — €49.99/month. 200 GB shared data, unlimited calls and SMS.
- **Data SIM 30GB** — €14.99/month. 30 GB data only (no voice or SMS), ideal for
  tablets and connected devices.

Postpaid plans are month-to-month with no minimum commitment. You can upgrade or
downgrade your plan once per billing cycle from the HexaMobile app.

## Changing your plan
Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. There is no fee
to change plans. Upgrades can be applied immediately on request; downgrades always
apply from the next cycle.
