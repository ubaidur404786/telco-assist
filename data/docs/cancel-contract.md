# Cancelling your HexaMobile contract

## Postpaid plans
HexaMobile postpaid plans have no minimum commitment, so you can cancel at any time.

1. Open the HexaMobile app and go to **Account > Manage subscription**.
2. Select **Cancel my plan**.
3. Choose your cancellation date (immediate, or the end of the current billing cycle).
4. Confirm. You will receive an email confirmation within 24 hours.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle unless you
choose an immediate cancellation. You are billed for the current cycle in full;
HexaMobile does not pro-rate the final month.

## Prepaid plans
There is nothing to cancel on a prepaid plan. Simply stop topping up and the line
becomes inactive after 90 days without a top-up.

## Keeping your number (portability)
If you are switching to another operator, do **not** cancel first. Instead, request
your RIO number by calling 3179 from your HexaMobile line, then give it to your new
operator, who will handle the port and the cancellation for you. Number portability
in France completes within 1 to 3 business days.

## Early termination fees
There are no early termination fees on any HexaMobile plan.
