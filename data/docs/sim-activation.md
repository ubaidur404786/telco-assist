# Activating your SIM or eSIM

## Physical SIM
1. Insert the SIM card into your phone.
2. Open the HexaMobile app (or go to activate.hexamobile.fr) and sign in.
3. Enter the 6-digit activation code printed on the SIM holder.
4. Restart your phone. Activation usually completes within 10 minutes and can take
   up to 4 hours.

## eSIM
1. In the HexaMobile app, go to **Account > Activate eSIM**.
2. Scan the QR code shown on screen with the device you want to activate, or tap
   **Activate on this device** if you are already on your phone.
3. Follow your phone's prompts to add the mobile plan.

eSIM is available on all postpaid plans at no extra cost. Prepaid eSIM is available
on Prepaid Plus only.

## New line vs. replacement SIM
- **New line**: your number is assigned during activation.
- **Replacement SIM** (lost, damaged, or new phone): your existing number and plan
  transfer automatically once the new SIM is activated; the old SIM stops working
  immediately.

## Troubleshooting activation
If activation has not completed after 4 hours, toggle Airplane mode on and off,
then restart. If it still fails, open a device ticket in the app with your SIM's
serial number (ICCID).
