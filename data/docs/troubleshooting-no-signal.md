# Troubleshooting: no signal or no mobile data

Work through these steps in order. Most signal problems are fixed by the first three.

## 1. Quick checks
- Toggle **Airplane mode** on, wait 10 seconds, then off.
- Restart your phone.
- Make sure you are not in a known low-coverage area or a building with thick walls.

## 2. Check for a network outage
Open the HexaMobile app and go to **Network > Coverage & outages**, or check the
coverage map, to see whether there is a reported incident in your area. Planned
maintenance is announced there in advance.

## 3. No mobile data specifically
- Confirm mobile data is switched on in your phone settings.
- Check you have not reached a capped plan's data limit for the month (data stops
  on prepaid; capped postpaid plans keep working but add €2/GB overage).
- Verify the **APN** is set to `hexa.mobile`. On most phones this configures
  automatically; you can reset it under **Settings > Mobile network > Access Point
  Names**.

## 4. Still no signal?
- Re-seat your physical SIM, or re-activate your eSIM.
- Test the SIM in another phone to rule out a handset fault.
- If several nearby HexaMobile users are also affected, it is likely a cell outage;
  service is usually restored within a few hours.

If none of this works, open a **network** ticket in the app. Include your city and
the time the problem started so support can correlate it with cell data.
